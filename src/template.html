Questions people ask us

Quick answers

Is it $25 every month?

No. $25 pays for your first month only. One month after you join, you pay your membership's regular price: Bogey $99, Par $175, Birdie $250, Eagle $299 or Junior $125 (all plus tax).

What happens after I pay the $25?

You get a receipt from Square by email. Then book a bay online and use the door code from your booking to get in. You can start playing right away.

Before your second month starts, Square emails you a secure link to save your card. Open it and save your card so your membership keeps going.

Do I sign a contract?

No contract. Your membership renews each month until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Email 1872gc.gtx@gmail.com or tell the front desk at least 15 days before your next bill. You keep playing until the end of the month you already paid for. Payments already made aren't refunded.

Is tax included in the price?

No. Texas sales tax (8.25%) is added at checkout. Example: $25 becomes $27.06, and Par's $175 becomes $189.44.

Who counts as "household" on Birdie and Eagle?

Up to 2 adults plus kids under 18 who live at the same address. We may ask for proof of address.

How do guests (friends) work?

Birdie: 1 friend plays free each day. Eagle: 3 friends play free each day. Every other guest is $10 per guest, per hour, paid before you play. You need to be there the whole time your guests are.

Guests are welcome any time we're open, except during events like Thursday Night Skins, leagues and tournaments.

Can I use the $25 deal on a 6- or 12-month prepay?

No. The $25 first month is for monthly plans only. Prepay is a separate way to save.

Can I switch to a different membership later?

Yes. Tell us and the change starts on your next billing date.

I'm already a member. Does any of this change for me?

The $25 deal is for new members. If you're already a member, you keep the rate you pay today.

Legacy 20 (our original Mulligan and Founding members): you now pay $225/mo automatically. Nothing to do. You keep 3 bookings on the calendar at a time of up to 3 hours each, your drink perk, and Eagle coaching perks: 1 free lesson and 1 clinic every month plus 15% off lesson packs.

Can I sign up in person or over the phone?

Yes. Stop by the front desk or call 512-887-2881 and we'll set you up.
