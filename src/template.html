Got questions?

Quick answers

Is it $25 every month?

No. $25 is only your first month. After that you pay your plan's normal price.

What happens after I pay?

You get a receipt by email. Book a bay and come play!

Before month 2, Square emails you a link to save your card. Do it so you can keep playing.

How do I quit?

Email 1872gc.gtx@gmail.com or tell the front desk 15 days before your next bill. No contract. No fee.

Is tax included?

No. Tax is 8.25%. So $25 is $27.06, and $175 is $189.44.

Can I bring friends?

Yes! Birdie: 1 friend free every day. Eagle: 3 friends free every day. Other friends are $10 each, per hour.

No guests during Skins, leagues or tournaments.

I'm already a member. Does this change anything?

No. You keep the price you pay now. The $25 deal is for new members.

Legacy 20 (our first Mulligan and Founding members): you now pay $225 a month on your own. You keep your 3 bookings and drink perk, and you get 1 free lesson and 1 group lesson every month, plus 15% off lesson packs.

Can I sign up in person?

Yes! Come see us or call 512-887-2881.
